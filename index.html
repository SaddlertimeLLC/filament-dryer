    How the estimate works

    Filament dries fast at first, then slows. The app fits your weigh-ins to w(t) = dry + water·e−t/τ, where t is time in the oven only (time out of the oven is not counted). This projects the fully-dry weight, so it can work out:

    • Moisture % = (current − dry weight) ÷ (dry weight − empty spool).
    • Time to target: when projected moisture drops to your target %.
    • Diminishing returns: when the loss over one weigh interval would be smaller than your scale can detect. Drying longer won't show any measurable change.

    You need at least 3 weigh-ins (including the start) for a projection. Early on, while the loss is still roughly a straight line, the projection is rough. It firms up as the curve bends. Target % values are practical rules of thumb, so adjust them to taste. A scale that reads to 0.1 g helps a lot.
